About VER+ Huellas
What are we measuring?

VER+ Huellas is VélezReyes+'s monthly impact report. Each edition brings together two types of stories. Learning Agenda covers our long-term bets and what we are discovering along the way. Success Stories features impact stories from our partner organizations and grantees. Not every team appears in every edition.
