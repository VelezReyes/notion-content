About VER+ Huellas
What are we measuring?

VER+ Huellas is VélezReyes+'s repository of evidence on impact. Updated monthly, it combines two types of stories. "Learning Agenda" summarizes the findings from the MEL exercises we are internally carrying out as part of the Stage-Based Learning Framework. "Success Stories" features impact stories from our partner organizations.
