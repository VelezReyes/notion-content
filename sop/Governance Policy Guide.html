Governance Policy Guide

How organizational policies are created, approved, published, and maintained. · Effective 2026

Organizational policies are the shared rules that define how VélezReyes+ and Vere work together. They apply to everyone on the team, regardless of role or country, and are reviewed regularly to stay current.

If you are looking for a specific policy, go to the Policy Directory tab. Every active policy links directly to its Notion Employee Hub summary.

Overview
What organizational policies are, what to expect from them, and how they stay current.
What is an organizational policy?

An organizational policy is a shared rule that applies to every team member at VélezReyes+ and Vere, regardless of role, seniority, or country. Policies exist to create consistency, protect everyone's rights, and make sure the company operates within legal and ethical boundaries.

Policies are different from team-level SOPs or project playbooks. A policy sets a company-wide standard. An SOP explains how to execute it in a specific context. Both matter, but one without the other is incomplete.

What counts as an organizational policy?
Any rule that applies to all team members across VélezReyes+ and Vere, including HR and talent policies. Team-specific SOPs, project-level procedures, client playbooks, and role-specific guidelines are out of scope and do not go through this process.
What every policy includes

Every published policy comes with four things. This is the standard you can expect whenever a new policy is announced.

01
Policy Document
The official rule, in standard format. Drafted by People & Operations and signed off by both CEOs before it is published.
02
Standard Operating Procedure
Step-by-step instructions for any repeatable process the policy involves. Included when a process is needed, not just rules.
03
Templates
Forms, checklists, or communication samples the policy requires, stored in the same Drive folder as the policy and SOP.
04
Notion Summary
A plain-language version on the Notion Employee Hub, with links to all Drive originals. This is your primary day-to-day reference.
Where to find everything
All policy documents and SOPs live in Google Drive at /8. Policies/[# and Policy Name]/. The easiest entry point is the Notion Employee Hub, which links everything together. Retired policies are preserved at /Policies/Retired/ with the date they were superseded.
Policies stay current

Every policy is reviewed at least once per year. If a review leads to material changes, both CEOs must approve the revision before it is republished and communicated to the team.

Reviews can also happen at any time if something significant changes: a new regulation, an organizational restructure, a new country or tool, or an incident that reveals a gap. You will always be notified when a policy changes.

Common questions
What is the difference between a policy and an SOP? ▾
A policy sets the rule: what applies, to whom, and why. A Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) explains how to execute the rule in practice, step by step. Most policies have a corresponding SOP. Both are published together and linked from the Notion Employee Hub.
Does a policy apply to me if I am a contractor or based in a different country? ▾
Organizational policies apply to all VélezReyes+ and Vere team members. Individual policies may specify variations based on employment type or country where local law requires it. If you are unsure how a specific policy applies to your situation, contact People & Operations.
Can I suggest a new policy or flag that something is missing? ▾
Yes. Any team member can raise the need for a new organizational policy. Reach out to the Director of People & Operations with the context: what gap you are seeing, who it affects, and why a company-wide standard would help. People & Operations will evaluate it and, if appropriate, initiate the process.
Can my manager create a team rule that overrides a published policy? ▾
No. Organizational policies apply company-wide and cannot be overridden by team-level decisions. Managers can create team-specific SOPs and guidelines for things not covered by an organizational policy, but they cannot create rules that contradict or bypass a published policy. If you believe there is a conflict, raise it with People & Operations.
How will I know when a policy changes? ▾
Any material change to a policy goes through the same approval and communication process as a new policy. You will be notified via Slack and at a team meeting or all-hands. Minor corrections (typos, broken links) may be updated quietly. If you want to verify the current version of a policy, the Notion Employee Hub always reflects the latest approved text.
