When it applies
Reimbursement is for exceptional situations only — not a parallel payment method.
The Deel Card is always first
All eligible business and travel expenses must be paid with your Deel Card. The Finance team preloads it with approved funds before every trip or purchase. Reimbursement only applies when the Deel Card genuinely could not be used. If the card was available and you chose not to use it, the expense may not be reimbursed.
The four situations where reimbursement applies
🚫
Technical issue at the point of sale
The Deel Card failed to process at the merchant even though it should be funded. Pay with personal funds, document the issue, and submit for reimbursement.
🚫
Merchant does not accept the card
Some vendors do not accept international or prepaid cards. Confirm this was the case and document it in your submission comment.
⚠️
Emergency while traveling
An urgent, unforeseen expense arises during a business trip and the Deel Card is unavailable or insufficient. Notify your manager as soon as possible.
✅
Pre-approved out-of-pocket expense
Your manager explicitly authorized you to pay with personal funds before the purchase — for example, a training course or language class. Written approval is required.

Expenses vs. Allowances in Deel

Deel distinguishes between two types of payments. Make sure you are selecting the right one when submitting.

Expenses
Reimbursement for actual cost
Must be supported by a receipt. You are reimbursed for exactly what you paid.
Allowances
Predefined fixed amount
Provided regardless of actual spending. May be subject to taxes. Not the same as a reimbursement.
When in doubt, ask first
If you are unsure whether an expense qualifies, contact the Admin Director before making the purchase. It is always better to confirm upfront than to submit a request that gets denied.
How to submit
Three phases. Each has a clear owner and a specific deadline. Do not skip Phase 1.
P1
Before you spend · Employee + Manager
Confirm and get pre-approval
Before making any out-of-pocket payment.
▾

Do not skip this phase. If you pay out of pocket without completing these steps, your reimbursement may be denied.

  1. Identify why the Deel Card cannot be used. The reason must fit one of the four valid scenarios: technical issue, merchant declined, emergency while traveling, or pre-approved out-of-pocket expense.
  2. Get written approval from your Direct Manager via email or Slack before making the payment. Capture their response — you will need it if the request is questioned.
  3. Note the justification. Write down the reason the Deel Card was not used. You will copy this into the Comments field in Deel when submitting.
  4. Pay with personal funds and keep every receipt — paper or digital — from the moment of payment.
No approval = no guarantee
Expenses paid out of pocket without prior manager approval are not automatically covered. The Admin Director may deny the request if no justification was documented.
P2
Submit in Deel · Employee · Within 3 days of expense
Log and submit your request
Log in to app.deel.com → Finance → Expenses.
▾
Single expense
  1. Go to Finance → Expenses and click Add item.
  2. Select the appropriate category: Expenses or Travel.
  3. Add one item per expense (one for the flight, one for the hotel, one per meal, one per Uber).
  4. Upload the receipt for each item. It must show date, vendor, amount, and currency.
  5. Select the correct expense category and add a comment describing the concept. State clearly why the Deel Card was not used and reference your manager's approval.
  6. Submit the report for approval.
Multiple expenses (trips or OPEX)

When submitting several expenses together, create a Grouped Report so the approver can review them as a set.

  1. Click Add New and select Trip/Grouped Report.
  2. Choose Business Trip for travel expenses, or Other (Non-travel) for OPEX expenses.
  3. Name the group using the format: Cost Center – Project – Type of Expense.
    e.g. “Education – Travel Expenses – OPEX” or “Education – Becas cohort 2026 – Direct Cost”
  4. Add individual expenses to the group using New Item or Existing Item.
  5. Review all items and submit the grouped report.
Currency & comments
Always submit receipts in the original currency shown on the receipt. Deel converts to your local currency automatically. Every expense item must have a descriptive comment — well-written comments speed up approval and simplify reconciliation.
Deadline
Submit within 3 calendar days of the expense and before the end of the month in which the expense occurred. Missing either condition may result in denial and the amount being withheld or deducted from your income.
P3
Review & Payroll · Manager → Admin Director → Accountant
Approval and payment
After submission. You track status in Deel.
▾
  1. Direct Manager review. Your manager reviews the submission for legitimacy, category accuracy, and budget. They approve or return it with comments.
  2. Admin Director approval. Validates receipts, checks compliance with the policy, and confirms the justification for not using the Deel Card.
  3. Finance processing. Once approved, the Accountant schedules the reimbursement for the next payroll cycle.
  4. Payroll cutoff: Approved on or before the 20th → included in the next payroll cycle. Approved after the 20th → processed in the following cycle.
If your request is denied
You will be notified via Deel and email with the reason. You may correct and resubmit where applicable. If the request remains denied after correction, appeal to the Admin Director via email with supporting documentation.

Request an itemized receipt showing vendor, date, and total. Covers coffee, breakfast, lunch, dinner, hotel food, and groceries for snacks.
Respect per-diem limits from the Travel Policy when traveling.

Hotels
Always request the invoice at check-out, even for prepaid stays. Must show the full breakdown: nights, rate, taxes, and total paid.
A booking confirmation is not enough — you need the check-out invoice.
✈️
Air tickets
Upload the airline's payment confirmation — not just the booking confirmation. Must show itinerary and total amount paid, including any changes or add-ons.
Booking confirmation ≠ payment confirmation. You need the latter.
📚
Training & Courses
Upload the payment receipt or invoice. Include your manager's pre-approval in the comment. Includes language class benefit (up to USD 80/month, cumulative, from Jan 2026).
📋
Other — Expense
Any eligible business expense that does not fit the categories above. Upload the receipt and add a clear, specific comment describing the concept and business purpose.
Lost or missing receipts
Protocol for lost or missing receipts ▾

Lost receipts may be accepted at management discretion. Habitual missing receipts may lead to policy violations. Follow these steps within 3 calendar days of the expense:

  1. Write a brief explanation of why the receipt is missing.
  2. Gather supporting evidence: bank or credit card statement showing the transaction, confirmation emails, invoices, or payment confirmations from the vendor.
  3. Submit the explanation and evidence to the Finance team within 3 calendar days of the expense.
  4. Await Admin Director review. They will evaluate and approve the lost-receipt documentation before the reimbursement is processed.
Repeated missing receipts
Habitual missing receipts escalate to the Admin Director and may result in a formal policy violation.

Issues & FAQ
Common questions and what to do when something goes wrong.
Frequently asked questions
When should I request a reimbursement instead of using the Deel Card? ▾
Only when you genuinely could not use the Deel Card: the merchant did not accept it, there was a technical issue, you were in an emergency, or your manager pre-approved an out-of-pocket expense. In all other cases, use the Deel Card. If the card was available and you chose not to use it, the expense may not be reimbursed.
What if I forget to upload my receipts within 3 days? ▾
Upload them as soon as possible and explain the delay in the comment field. Contact the Admin Director to inform them of the situation. Repeated late submissions may lead to policy violations. If receipts are not submitted within 3 calendar days and before month-end, the expense may be denied and the amount may be withheld or deducted from your income.
When will I receive my reimbursement? ▾
Approved reimbursements are processed through payroll. If your request is fully approved on or before the 20th of the month, it will be included in the next payroll cycle. Requests approved after the 20th will be processed in the following cycle.
My receipt is in a different currency. What do I submit? ▾
Always submit the receipt in its original currency. Deel will show the exchange rate and convert to your local currency automatically. Do not convert amounts manually.
I lost a receipt. What do I do? ▾
Follow the Lost Receipt protocol in the Receipt Guide tab: write a brief explanation, gather supporting evidence (bank statement, vendor email, payment confirmation), and submit both to the Finance team within 3 calendar days. The Admin Director will review and decide whether to accept the documentation.
My request was denied. What are my options? ▾
You will receive the denial reason via Deel and email. Review it carefully:
  • If the issue is correctable (incomplete receipt, missing comment), fix it and resubmit.
  • If you believe the denial is incorrect, appeal to the Admin Director via email with supporting evidence.
  • If the denial stands after appeal, the expense will not be reimbursed.
