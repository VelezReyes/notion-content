Paid Time Off Guide

30 calendar days/year + local law. Two platforms, one framework. · Effective April 2026

Our organization provides 30 calendar days of vacation per year to all employees, plus maternity, paternity, and other leave types. The standard is company-wide, regardless of country, and combines the best available LATAM benchmarks with each country's local labor law.

All time off is managed through two platforms: Deel for days mandated by your country's labor law, and Day Off for the company top-up and additional benefits. Knowing which platform to use, and in what order, is the foundation of this guide. The platform split is shown below, and the Country Rules tab explains how it applies to each country.

Team coverage & overlapping requests

Employees within the same team may not take vacation during overlapping dates. Team coverage must be maintained at all times.

If two or more team members request the same period and neither request has been approved yet, priority goes to the employee who has used fewer vacation days in the current year.

Plan and communicate your vacation requests as early as possible — the earlier you submit, the better your chances of getting the dates you want.

D
Platform · app.deel.com
Requesting via Deel
Vacation (legal), sick leave, bereavement, maternity/paternity legal portion
▾
  1. Log in to Deel with your username and password at app.deel.com.
  2. Open Time Off. In the side menu, click Time Off. Review your balances (Total, Used, Remaining) in the Balances tab.
  3. Start a new request. Click Submit new request (or Request time off) in the top-right corner.
  4. Select the leave type:
    • Vacation · for annual vacation (legal portion).
    • Sick leave · for medical absences (attach medical certificate).
    • Other leave · for marriage, bereavement, maternity/paternity legal portion.
  5. Set the dates. Enter Start and End date. Full days only; no half-days for vacation. Enter each split period as a separate date range.
  6. Add notes. Include context, e.g. "Annual vacation · coordinated with [Manager] on [date]."
  7. Attach documents using the Attach file option: medical certificate, birth certificate, marriage certificate, etc.
  8. Submit. Click Submit request. Track status (Pending / Approved / Rejected) in the Requests tab.
Brazil only · Abono Pecuniário
To convert up to 10 vacation days to cash (33% constitutional bonus applies), add the note: "Solicito Abono Pecuniário · venda de 10 dias." The request must be submitted at least 15 days before the end of the acquisition period.
DO
Platform · day-off.app
Requesting via Day Off
Vacation top-up, marriage, family emergency, maternity/paternity top-up, year-end shutdown
▾
  1. Log in to Day Off via the web or mobile app using your work account at day-off.app.
  2. Go to Time Off / Requests and click Request Time Off (or the + button).
  3. Fill in the details:
    • Type: Vacation top-up, Year-end Shutdown, Family Emergency, Marriage/Honeymoon, or Other.
    • Dates: Start and End dates.
    • Duration: Full day or half day.
    • Notes: Add context. If this is a vacation top-up, reference your Deel request.
  4. Submit. Your Direct Manager receives the request for approval. Track status via the app or email notifications.
Split leave · order matters
For vacation (Colombia / Costa Rica) and maternity/paternity top-ups: 1. Submit Deel first (legal portion). 2. Then create the Day Off request for the top-up portion. In Day Off Notes, reference the Deel request: "Top-up · Deel request submitted on [date]."

Special procedure · Deel
Sick Leave
Medical certificate required for all sick leave, regardless of duration
▾
  1. Notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible · same day or next morning.
  2. Obtain a medical certificate from a licensed physician covering the absence dates.
  3. Open Deel → Time Off → Submit new request. Select Type = Sick leave. Enter Start and End dates per the certificate.
  4. Attach the certificate using the Attach file option in Deel.
  5. Notify the Director of Staff and Operations by email and attach the medical certificate.
Hospitalization or prolonged illness
Contact the Director of Staff and Operations for guidance on social security notifications and any additional documentation required by your country.
👶
Special procedure · Both platforms
Maternity & Paternity Leave
126 days (maternity) · 60 days (paternity) · Deel + Day Off consecutive
▾
  1. Notify early. Inform your Direct Manager and the Director of Staff and Operations at least 30 days before the expected start date whenever possible.
  2. Gather documentation:
    • Brazil: Birth certificate (post-birth). Maternity may start up to 28 days before expected birth with medical certificate.
    • Colombia: Medical statement for pregnancy + birth certificate.
    • Costa Rica: Medical certificate + birth certificate per local rules.
    • Uruguay: Registered with BPS · submit medical and birth documentation.
  3. Submit in Deel for the legal-minimum portion. Select Type = Other leave (maternity/paternity where available). Attach documents.
  4. Submit in Day Off for the company top-up portion · consecutive with the Deel portion to form a single continuous leave. Reference the Deel request in Notes.
  5. Complete handover (see Handover SOP below) before the leave begins.
  6. Coordinate return-to-work with the Director of Staff and Operations at least 2 weeks before the planned return.
📋
Required · 3+ consecutive working days
Pre-Departure Handover
Required before any leave of 3 or more consecutive working days
▾

Send a handover email to your direct supervisor and any team members responsible for ongoing activities. Subject line: Handover · [Your Name] · Out [start date] to [end date].

Your handover must cover:

  • Dates: Start date, end date, and first day back.
  • Availability: Whether you will check email at all; if so, frequency and urgency threshold.
  • Ongoing projects: Each project with current status, next steps, and the owner during your absence.
  • Time-sensitive tasks: Tasks due during your absence and who will cover them.
  • Open decisions: Any decisions pending your input and how they should be handled.
  • Calendar: Add the time-off period to your shared calendar and copy the CEO, Director of Staff and Operations, and relevant team.
  • Out-of-office responder: Set up an automatic email reply with your return date and an alternate contact.

Retroactive or Advance Request
Registering time off after it was taken, or requesting days before the accrual period
▾
  1. Obtain prior approval in writing (email) from your Direct Manager before submitting anything in any platform.
  2. Notify the Director of Staff and Operations · forward the manager's approval and explain the reason.
  3. Submit the request in the applicable platform (Deel for legal days / Day Off for top-up) for the past or advance dates. Fill in dates and type as usual.
  4. Add justification in the Notes field using the template below.
  5. Attach supporting documentation (manager approval email, medical certificate, etc.).
  6. Follow up with the Director of Staff and Operations confirming the request was submitted.

Carryover rules

Legal vacation (Deel): carryover follows your country's local labor law · see the Deel article for your country in the table above.

Additional vacation (Day Off): carryover follows the same parameters that apply to legal vacation in your country.

Year-end collective shutdown: cannot be carried over · these are fixed dates for that year.

Marriage / Family Emergency: cannot be carried over.

Blackout periods
Leadership Team members cannot take vacations the week before or during board meetings or off-sites · especially in September (meeting with David and Mariel). Non-leadership employees should coordinate with their manager to avoid critical project periods. Exceptions require CEO approval.
Multiple requests for the same period

Employees within the same team may not take vacation during overlapping dates — adequate team coverage must be maintained at all times.

If two or more team members request the same period and neither request has been approved yet, priority goes to the employee who has used fewer vacation days in the current year.

Plan and communicate your vacation requests as early as possible to minimize conflicts and ensure better coordination within the team.

FAQ & Edge Cases
Common questions and what to do when something goes wrong.
How far in advance do I need to request vacation? ▾
At least 1 week before the start date. For longer blocks (more than 10 working days) or maternity/paternity leave, request 30 days or more in advance whenever possible. The sooner you notify the team, the easier it is to plan coverage.
Why do I need to use Deel before Day Off for vacation? ▾
The legal vacation balance is a protected entitlement under your country's labor law · it must be used (or paid out at termination). The Day Off balance is a discretionary company benefit that is forfeited when employment ends. Using legal days first ensures your statutory rights are fully exercised before drawing on the benefit.
I'm in Brazil (or Panama). Do I need Day Off for vacation at all? ▾
No. Brazil and Panama both have a legal minimum of 30 calendar days, which already matches the VélezReyes+ company standard. There is no additional top-up balance, so you use Deel only for vacation.
Can I sell back unused vacation days (Abono Pecuniário)? ▾
Brazil only. You may convert up to 10 vacation days to cash. The 33% constitutional bonus still applies. To request it, add the note "Solicito Abono Pecuniário · venda de 10 dias" in your Deel request, at least 15 days before the end of the acquisition period.
Can I take the year-end collective shutdown days in a different period? ▾
No. When the CEO activates the year-end shutdown for a given year, it is a fixed, company-wide period. The days cannot be moved, cashed out, or carried over. If no shutdown is activated that year, the 5 additional days are simply not granted.
What if my Deel Card doesn't work while traveling and I need to use sick leave · which platform? ▾
Sick leave is always submitted through Deel only · regardless of your country or situation. It is a legally protected leave type governed by local labor law. Notify your manager and submit the certificate as soon as you are able.
My marriage is coming up. How do I use the 5 days? ▾
Request the 5 days through Day Off. They may start up to 1 week before the wedding and be taken any time up to 1 year after. Request at least 30 days in advance when possible. Upload a marriage certificate once you have it · Day Off handles the documentation.
What documentation do I need for each leave type? ▾
  • Sick leave: Medical certificate (always required, regardless of duration).
  • Maternity/Paternity: Birth certificate or medical documentation per country (see Country Rules tab).
  • Marriage: Marriage certificate (attach after the wedding).
  • Bereavement: Documentation per local labor law.
  • Vacation: No documentation required unless retroactive or advance request.
  • Retroactive/Advance: Manager approval email + any relevant supporting document.
Two of us want the same vacation dates and neither request is approved yet. Who gets priority? ▾
The employee who has used fewer vacation days in the current year gets priority. This is why planning and communicating vacation early is strongly encouraged · it prevents conflicts and allows for better team coordination.
I need more time off than the policy allows. What are my options? ▾
Any time off beyond what this policy covers must be negotiated directly with your Direct Manager and the CEO. Any agreement must clearly state whether the time is paid or unpaid, the employee's responsibilities during the absence (if any), and how the time will be recorded. Exceptions require CEO approval.
