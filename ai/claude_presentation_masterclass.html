What What is a Skill? ▾

A Skill is a .zip file you upload once to your Claude account. It acts as a silent briefing — your brand rules, colors, and methodology — available in any chat by typing / and selecting it. No Projects, no re-uploading, no setup per conversation.

The VER+ and VERE Presentation Skills contain: brand color system, slide layout rules, design parameters, and the full presentation methodology. Claude uses all of it to output a .pptx that looks like ours.

Install How to install it ▾
1

Download the Skill file

Use the download buttons at the top — pick the one that matches your brand. You'll get a .zip file. Save it to your Desktop.

→ Do not unzip or rename it.

2

Go to Settings → Capabilities → Skills

In claude.ai, click your profile icon → Settings → Capabilities → scroll to Skills → click Manage Skills.

3

Upload the .zip

Click + → Upload → select the .zip file. Wait until it appears in your skills list.

→ Upload VER+ and VERE as separate skills — not together.

4

Activate with / in any chat

Open any chat, type / and select the skill from the menu. Claude is now briefed and ready to build your presentation.

→ Do this at the start of every new presentation chat. Takes 2 seconds.

Both brands?
Upload both skills separately. When starting a deck, activate the one that matches — VER+ for VélezReyes+, VERE for VERE. Never activate both in the same chat.
Use How to use it ▾
Step 1 Give Claude context first ▾

Tell Claude what the presentation is about: audience, goal, tone, and roughly how many slides. The Skill handles everything else — brand, colors, layouts.

"Using this skill, I need a 12-slide deck for a client kickoff. Audience: their leadership team. Goal: align on scope. Tone: structured and warm."

Step 2 Paste the content and let Claude build ▾

Paste your content — notes, bullet points, data, a document — and Claude starts generating directly using the brand rules from the Skill.

If the content is very raw and you want to review the structure before it builds, add this to your prompt: "Before generating, show me the slide-by-slide outline for my approval." Claude will pause, show you the structure, and wait for your go-ahead.

→ Max 40 words on screen per slide. Mention it if you have a lot of content and want the rest in speaker notes.

Step 3 Download and adjust ▾

Claude generates the .pptx and gives you a download link. Open it in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

→ Something looks off? Describe the issue — Claude fixes that slide without rebuilding the whole deck.

The Prompt
One prompt that activates the full workflow — from context to approved outline to generated file. Type /, activate your skill, then paste this. Fill in the brackets and follow Claude's lead.
Before you paste
Type / in the chat and activate your VER+ or VERE Presentation Skill first. Without it, Claude won't have the brand rules loaded.
Master prompt — copy and fill in the brackets
Using this skill, build a presentation with the following: Audience: [Who will see this] Goal: [What should they think, feel, or do after seeing it] Tone: [e.g. confident / warm / direct] Slides: [Approximate number] Content: [Paste your content here — notes, bullet points, data, anything you have.]
Optional — add this line if your content is very raw
Before generating, show me the slide-by-slide outline for my approval.
How it works
With the skill active, Claude already knows the brand — you only provide the context and content. It generates directly. The optional outline line is only for when your content is very rough and you want to review the structure before Claude builds anything.
