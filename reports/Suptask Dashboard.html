Official SLA (VélezReyes+ Suptask Guide, business days only)
Critical: 1 business day · High: up to 4 business days · Medium: up to 7 business days · Low: no fixed date / best effort. Weekends don't count toward these windows.
Due Diligence & Legal are marked separately
These two forms depend on external parties (trust and/or lawyers) once escalated — resolution timing is outside internal control, so open tickets in these forms are tagged "External dependency" instead of "Past SLA."

Monthly snapshots
Month-over-month history. Each snapshot freezes the numbers at the moment it was generated.
How this works on a static, GitHub-hosted page
This page has no backend or database, so a snapshot can't save itself automatically. The history lives as a plain JSON array committed in the file's source code (see snapshots-data near the top) — it is not stored in cookies or localStorage, so it isn't tied to any one person's browser. Once a new entry is added and the file is pushed to GitHub, everyone who opens the page sees the same updated history.

In practice: whenever you share a new monthly CSV export, that update (including adding the new snapshot entry) is done as part of processing the file — no manual editing needed on your side unless you want to.
