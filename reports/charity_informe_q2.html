Methodology

Managed means the team's recommendation work reached a D&M decision, approved, resolved by disbursement, or resolved by rejection, and the case is actively moving forward, even if it is currently blocked somewhere in downstream compliance execution such as Trust review, ID verification, or missing documents from a third party.
